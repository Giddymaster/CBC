Questions schools ask

Is Shulenest built for the CBC curriculum?

Yes. Reports show competency levels — Exceeding, Meeting, Approaching and Below Expectation — per learning area, seeded from the MoE catalogue, from Pre-Primary through Senior School.

Can parents pay school fees through Shulenest?

Yes. Fees are itemised by vote head, and parents pay by M-Pesa against their child's admission number. The office sees a live register with balances, and report forms carry the fee balance and next term's fee.

Does it work without reliable internet?

Yes. Score entry and registers are saved on the device when the connection drops and sync automatically once it returns — so a teacher can mark a class in a room with no signal.

Can we message all parents at once?

Yes. Send a bulk SMS or WhatsApp notice to the whole school, one class, or only families with a fee balance, with a preview of who it reaches before sending.

Is our data private from other schools?

Every school's records are isolated. A parent sees only their own children; staff see only their own school; and uploaded files are served through private, expiring links, never a public folder.
